# WerkZ Data Rights & Contract Wording Guide

**Date:** 2026-10-02  
**Status:** drafting guardrail, not final legal wording

## Core concern

“Data ownership” is often too imprecise for WerkZ contracts.

Customer records may simultaneously involve:
- personal-data rights under GDPR;
- copyright in individual works;
- database rights;
- trade secrets;
- confidentiality obligations;
- third-party licences;
- factual data with no standalone exclusive property right.

## Contract model

Use defined categories instead of blanket ownership claims:

### Customer Data
Information/content supplied by or on behalf of the customer, or generated from customer operational activity and allocated contractually to that category.

Contract should address:
- authority to provide/process;
- purpose-limited processing rights for WerkZ;
- confidentiality;
- export/return;
- retention/deletion;
- legal retention exceptions;
- third-party rights.

### WerkZ Service Data
Examples:
- service telemetry;
- security logs;
- billing/contract metadata;
- aggregated operational metrics.

Define separately and apply minimisation/privacy rules.

### Derived/AI output
Do not automatically call all output “WerkZ data” or “customer-owned data”.
Define:
- source inputs;
- generated result;
- provider terms;
- user/customer use rights;
- third-party/IP caveats;
- retention and audit metadata.

## GDPR correction

Article 30 GDPR concerns the **record of processing activities** (Verzeichnis von Verarbeitungstätigkeiten), not a generic “rights register”.

Reference:
https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN-DE/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32016R0679

## Database-right caution

A customer database does not become a WerkZ database-right asset merely because WerkZ hosts or processes it.

Assess §87a investment and the actual database maker separately.

Reference:
https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/urhg/__87a.html
