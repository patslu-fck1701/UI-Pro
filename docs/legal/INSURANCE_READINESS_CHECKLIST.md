# WerkZ Insurance Readiness Checklist

**Status:** pre-sales risk-transfer checklist. Insurance is not a substitute for security, contracts or legal compliance.

Before normal paid production rollout, compare actual insurer/broker terms for:

## Core cover
- [ ] Betriebshaftpflicht / Berufshaftpflicht / IT-Haftpflicht appropriate to actual activities
- [ ] Vermögensschäden from software/service errors
- [ ] data-loss and restoration costs
- [ ] privacy/security incident response
- [ ] third-party claims arising from cyber incidents
- [ ] defence/legal costs
- [ ] subcontractor/cloud-provider related scenarios
- [ ] worldwide/EU territorial scope matching customers

## Important exclusions / conditions
- [ ] contractual liability exclusions
- [ ] guarantees/SLA penalties exclusions
- [ ] known vulnerabilities / unsupported software exclusions
- [ ] ransomware/social-engineering exclusions
- [ ] minimum MFA/backup/patching requirements
- [ ] outsourced/cloud service conditions
- [ ] retroactive date / prior acts
- [ ] deductible/self-retention
- [ ] incident notification deadlines

## Evidence to retain
- insurer/broker;
- policy number;
- insured activities;
- limits/sub-limits;
- deductible;
- effective/renewal dates;
- major exclusions;
- required security controls;
- incident contact.

## Marketing rule

Do not state “insured” or advertise a coverage amount until a policy is actually bound and the relevant scope is confirmed.
